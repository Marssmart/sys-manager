Sys Manager menu bar guide

Pick one direction

Ten takes on the Sys Manager menu bar utility. Each shows the menu bar strip with the status item, and its dropdown with two views. Disk Cleaner lists the largest files and folders, re-sorting live while the scan runs. Process Viewer lists processes with CPU and memory; click a row to inspect it, and Kill ends it in one click.

Mock data only. Sizes use decimal units, like Finder. Processes owned by root or system accounts are protected, so their Kill is disabled. The buttons at top right switch every variant at once; the tabs inside each dropdown switch one. Click a status item to open or close its dropdown.
